Read docs/PROJECT.md for what the product is, who the customer is, and the goal. If the customer is not named there, ask before starting. Tag every point observed (anyone could verify it) or opinion (your judgment). Return text only; the main session writes files.

# Competitor test
Include the customer's real alternatives, such as a spreadsheet, a human advisor, or a general-purpose AI.
For each capability: what the alternative does, what this product does, the evidence for both, whether the difference is substantive or cosmetic, and a specific improvement opportunity.
Do not copy competitor claims as facts without verification. Do not recommend rebuilding what the product already does unless there is a demonstrated gap.
