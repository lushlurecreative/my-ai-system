# Banned patterns (project data, kept on update)

Patterns the owner has rejected. A page that uses one is failed by the critic. Add a line whenever the owner rejects a site, naming the pattern.

- Fixed layout templates or presets for customer sites.
- A scrolling strip of offerings near the top.
- A giant name as the whole first screen with one photo and one button, and nothing else.
- Empty picture boxes, stock photos, or invented content to fill space.
