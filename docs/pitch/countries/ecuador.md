# Country profile: Ecuador
## Beliefs to answer
- Many owners think being on Instagram and WhatsApp is enough. It is not. The pitch answers this directly: Instagram and WhatsApp are not found by someone searching on Google, Bing or an AI assistant; a website is.
- Many owners think a website will bring them little. The pitch answers: more places you appear means more eyes and, very likely, more customers (never guaranteed), and credibility: when someone searches for you, you appear in five ways instead of two.
- Owners rarely know what Google Maps, Google Business and Analytics do for them. The website is the key that connects them; Instagram and WhatsApp stay, they are not replaced.
## Channels people use
- Instagram for marketing, WhatsApp for bookings and questions, Facebook. Link-in-bio pages and shared drives often stand in for a website.
## Search engines and maps people use
- Google and Google Maps lead. Bing and AI assistants also deliver websites. Search numbers for a small city may be low; use the real number and say what it is.
## Directories and marketplaces
- Google Business listings, Rappi and PedidosYa for delivery, local directories.
## Tone
- Warm, respectful and direct. Say plainly what we noticed, from facts only. Criticise the setup, never the person. A compliment only when a real strength backs it.
## Holidays and seasons
- Valentine's Day (14 February), Mother's Day (second Sunday of May), Carnival, All Souls' (2 November), Christmas. Use real dates as reasons to write.
## Language
- Messages are written in English first and translated to Ecuadorian Spanish.
