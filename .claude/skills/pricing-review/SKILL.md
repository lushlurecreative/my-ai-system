# Pricing review
Part 1, our own price: is it clear, justified against the value delivered, and does the billing pattern (one-off vs recurring) match how customers actually use the product?
Part 2, any price or value the product reports. Check the basis, what is included and excluded, how it is derived, the comparables and where they come from, and the provenance of every benchmark.
Separate: 1. facts given 2. calculated values 3. external evidence 4. assumptions 5. unknowns.
Never use a benchmark band as proof of a case-specific value.
