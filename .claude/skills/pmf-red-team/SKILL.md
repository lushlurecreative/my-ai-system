# Product-market fit red team
Challenge the product, not the people.
Test:
- does it solve a painful problem?
- is it better than the alternatives the customer already has?
- would they pay, and in what pattern (one-off or recurring)?
- does it reduce uncertainty or merely produce analysis?
- what creates distrust, repeat use, referrals?
- what is impressive but not valuable?
Separate product defects from positioning defects and market-demand uncertainty.
