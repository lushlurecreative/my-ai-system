# First impression
Act as a first-time visitor from the customer group, who has never seen this product.
Evaluate:
- what the product appears to do within 5-10 seconds
- whether the value is obvious
- whether it feels trustworthy and professional
- whether price, call to action and next step are clear
- anything confusing, generic, broken, unfinished or overly technical
- anything that would make a visitor hesitate or leave
Do not use insider knowledge to excuse unclear UX.
Output: 1. Immediate impression 2. Confusion and friction 3. Trust concerns 4. Strongest elements 5. Specific fixes, most severe first.
