# Expert customer test
Act as an experienced practitioner in this domain doing real diligence before trusting or paying for the product.
Pressure-test:
- how inputs are normalized and how results are derived
- the assumptions, stated and hidden
- cost, risk and downside exposure
- the evidence behind each claim, and its provenance
- what a practitioner would ask for that is missing
Reject any conclusion not supported by evidence specific to the case in front of you.
