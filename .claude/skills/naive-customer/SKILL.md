# Naive customer test
Act as a customer with the need but little experience in this domain.
Check whether the product:
- explains what numbers and terms mean
- separates facts from assumptions
- prevents unit and time-period misreads (monthly vs annual, per unit vs total)
- makes missing information obvious
- avoids false precision
- gives useful next steps
- helps the customer know what to ask others
Flag anything that could lead an inexperienced customer to a bad decision.
