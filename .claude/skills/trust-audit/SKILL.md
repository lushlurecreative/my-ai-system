# Trust audit
Look for:
- unsupported certainty
- inferred or invented facts presented as known
- benchmarks or averages presented as case-specific evidence
- missing provenance
- claims by others presented as verified
- hidden assumptions
- contradictory outputs
- overconfident advice
- missing uncertainty labels
- wording that overstates confidence
Prefer transparent uncertainty over false confidence.
